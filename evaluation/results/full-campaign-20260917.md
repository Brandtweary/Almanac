## Corrected background follow-up

The original 30 background cases remain diagnostic because retrieval-scope and typed-speech instructions conflicted with their expectations. They are excluded from comparative totals, and the version 3 rerun is reported separately rather than overwriting them.

Of 27 transport-complete corrected trajectories, 7 meet all frozen content clauses and 5 also complete every mechanical check. Six of the seven content successes reject the erroneous historical speech pairing while preserving the intentional title and alias. The seventh, Qwen3.6's voice audit, is affected by an evaluator dictionary-loader defect and does not complete cleanly. Three voice trajectories encounter this loader error: Qwen3.6, Qwen3.8 and GLM. Their inability to persist an automatic rule is not model evidence. The loader is fixed and regression-tested for relocated snapshots with symlinked dependencies; no paid replay was performed during adjudication.

Muse correctly rejects the bad historical pairing and repairs the stale Tuesday meeting description, but misses the raw speech garble. It also repairs the rack label, aliases and tile facts while wrongly crediting `no_stem` with reducing an Arms/arm collision. The actual matcher still retrieves Arms for arm. No measured candidate fully satisfies that matching-repair case. These are concrete limits on autonomous maintenance, not evidence that every background operation fails.

The Gemma26 follow-up is more informative than its original provider rejection: 15 of 27 measured research answers meet the content rubric, only 5 also pass workflow checks, and 4 of 5 character cases pass. Its basic personal-fact/provenance cases and both summary cases can work, while dedicated maintenance repeatedly remains incomplete. This is useful alternative-model evidence, not a reason to replace Muse.

## Evidence and limitations

[Case-level adjudication and receipt identities](full-campaign-20260917.json) accompanies this report. Reviews are AI-authored semantic judgments against frozen questions, evidence and actual outputs, not human-certified ground truth. Raw provider traces are retained separately; hashes identify them but do not make the report independently reproducible without those traces. No new provider calls were made to produce this review.

All research uses controlled, source-grounded scenarios. These counts do not measure arbitrary library retrieval, sustained long-context behavior, deployed local quantization, or integrated audit→memory→summary reliability. One sample per case/provider is not a stability estimate. Local checks, actual-library admission and deployment checks remain separate evidence.

[Four local current-prompt checks](local-prompt-checks-20260917.json) separately pass sparse personal-fact storage, summary correction, practical-source uncertainty and conversational character on the installed quantized candidate. These exposed synthetic scenarios verify the later prompt amendments without rewriting the hosted results or qualifying all autonomous maintenance.
